# Yaw

Two related headings:

- **Orbit yaw** — horizontal half of [orbit](./orbit.md) from Option (`⌥`)+two-finger drag (shared middle-mouse velocity channel with pitch).
- **Rotation** — two-finger **twist** on the trackpad. Turns camera heading or, while placing / relocating, the ghost. Does not use the orbit velocity channel; starting rotation hard-handoffs leftover orbit coast.

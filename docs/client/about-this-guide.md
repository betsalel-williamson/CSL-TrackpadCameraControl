# About this guide

**Prepared for:** the personas in [Personas](./personas.md) — Cities: Skylines I players who use a trackpad and want map-app-like camera control without a three-button mouse.

**Product:** Trackpad Camera Control

This client guide covers install, feel presets, and Options / hot tuning for the **ship Maps+ surface**. Player-visible UI stays at [UI parity](../glossary/ui-parity.md) with the v1 contract (look and interactions). Unfinished modules (CAD style switcher, Contacts capture, Assist chrome) are omitted from the ship DLL — they are not troubleshot here.

Contributor setup lives under `docs/developer/`.

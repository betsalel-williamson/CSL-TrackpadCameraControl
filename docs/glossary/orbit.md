# Orbit

Rotate the camera around a pivot (yaw and pitch together) — the Cities: Skylines I middle-mouse drag feel.

**Activation (Maps+ / AppleKit):** Option (`⌥`)+two-finger drag. Pitch stays within vanilla **0–90°**. Once orbit starts, [orbit latch](./orbit-latch.md) holds until you lift your fingers. Orbit always uses the **current camera look-at** — selection, place/relocate ghost, and prior pivots do not re-home Target.

CAD three-finger orbit is behind `EnableCadGestureStyle`.

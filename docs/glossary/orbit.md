# Orbit

Rotate the camera around a pivot (yaw and pitch together) — the Cities: Skylines I middle-mouse drag feel.

**Activation (Maps+ / AppleKit):** Option (`⌥`)+two-finger drag. Pitch stays within Pitch min / max (**7–90°**). Once orbit starts, [orbit latch](./orbit-latch.md) holds until you lift your fingers. With a **selection**, the camera orbits **around that object**; with no selection, orbit uses the usual camera pivot.

CAD three-finger orbit is behind `EnableCadGestureStyle`.

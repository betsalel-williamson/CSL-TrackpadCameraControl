# Orbit

Camera pitch + yaw around the look-at pivot (same family as vanilla middle-mouse drag). Pitch follows the [apply constant](./apply-constant.md) **0°–90°**. Pivot is always the **current** look-at — no Target re-home to selection or place/relocate ghost.

**Maps+:** Option (`⌥`)+two-finger drag. Once orbit starts, [orbit latch](./orbit-latch.md) holds until fingers lift.

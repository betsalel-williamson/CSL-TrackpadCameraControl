# Feel preset

A saved or built-in **feel profile**: [Sensitivity](./sensitivity.md) values, orbit pitch min/max (7–90°), and other product-surface feel fields.

Built-in profiles **Slow**, **Default**, and **Fast** are immutable. The feel **dropdown** loads a profile on select; **Save as…** is the last entry and stores a named user profile. Editing while a preset is active moves the active identity to **New Preset** and autosaves there. **Reset to factory** restores the Default profile.

A feel preset is **not** a [gesture style](./gesture-style.md) (Maps+ vs CAD orbit trigger).

# Mod surface

The **Cities: Skylines I** stack layer: style binding table and Maps+ seed, [feel catalog (term)](./feel-catalog.md) and editor, Options/Debug hosts, Policy resolve, FeelMath, thin Cities adapters, and Harmony patches. Lives under `rewrite/mod`.

It consumes frames from the [gesture library](./gesture-library.md) and must not own AppKit P/Invoke. Full stack: features guide _Under the hood_ and ADR 0006.
